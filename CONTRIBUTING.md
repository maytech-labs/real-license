# Contributing to REAL

Thank you for your interest in improving the Reciprocal Ethical AI License!

## How to Contribute
- **Issue Tracker:** Use issues to report ambiguities, discuss legal edge cases, or suggest drafting improvements.
- **Pull Requests:** PRs updating the core `LICENSE.md` text should target draft versions (e.g., `REAL 1.1-draft`) or propose clear amendments in the discussion first.

*Note: Suggestions and feedback provided in issues or PRs are contributed under the understanding that they may be incorporated into future iterations of the license.*
